Smart. Stylish. Connected.

The All-New Suzuki Access 125 Bluetooth Enabled redefines urban mobility with its perfect blend of style, technology, and performance. Featuring advanced Bluetooth connectivity, this smart scooter keeps you connected while you ride.

With its 124cc engine delivering exceptional fuel efficiency of 48 kmpl and a host of premium features, the Access 125 is designed for the modern rider who values both performance and connectivity.

Bluetooth Connectivity

Turn-by-turn navigation, call alerts, and smartphone integration for a connected riding experience.

TFT Display

4.2-inch colored TFT display with crisp visuals and faster refresh rates for better readability.

Eco-Friendly

BS6 Phase 2B compliant engine with superior fuel efficiency and reduced emissions.

Safety First

LED headlamps, disc brakes, and advanced safety features for confident riding.

COLORED TFT DIGITAL CONSOLE

The new Access features a 4.2 inch colored TFT (Thin-Film Transistor) Digital Console. This modern display offers brighter, crisper, and has faster refresh rates than standard LCD displays thereby enabling better image quality, higher contrast ratios, and more accurate colors. It enhances the rider's experience by presenting essential information with clarity and visual appeal both in day & night.

Bluetooth® Enabled Digital Coloured TFT Cluster is available in Access Ride Connect TFT Edition and Bluetooth® Enabled Digital Monochrome Cluster is available in Access Ride Connect Edition.

GREATER TORQUE AT LOWER RPM

The engine produces greater torque at lower rpm. Maximum torque has also been increased and is now achieved at a lower rpm (5,500->5,000 rpm). This change contributes to enhancing fuel efficiency, and to improving the feel of acceleration and the pleasure of riding.

OPTIMIZED CVT SETTINGS

The finely tuned CVT transmission delivers satisfying acceleration at low rpm, without having to open the throttle excessively, while its settings are also effective in limiting fuel consumption. By matching the CVT settings to vehicle speed in the 30-60km/h range, the engine's rpm rises gradually to deliver a pleasing feeling much like that of a motorcycle.

IMPROVED COMBUSTION EFFICIENCY AND ENGINE PERFORMANCE

This is achieved by implementing changes to the camshaft profile. Specifically, the intake cam lifts higher to increase the amount of air intake, while the exhaust cam timing was adjusted to minimize overlap.

IDEAL COMBUSTION EFFICIENCY

Optimized intake port's shape and combustion chamber shape to maximizes the flow velocity of the air-fuel mixture entering the combustion chamber from intake port. Optimized combustion chamber design helps in achieving a high compression ratio that results in low fuel consumption and powerful engine output. This ranges from the application of a relatively long stroke to optimization of the combustion chamber shape, which, as described above, adopts an M-shaped squish area and banana-shaped intake port.

FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM & OPTIMIZED INTAKE VALVE SHAPE

The mounting angle of the injector is also optimized to for excellent combustion efficiency. Precise control over fuel delivery also makes engine starts easier and more reliable, even in cold weather. The engine produces far less emissions and provides a smoother riding experience, which is achieved without deteriorating the engine's performance.

Optimized intake valve shape: The design of the intake valve, with its two-layer chamfer and waist stem, further contributes to the overall efficiency of the intake system.

SUZUKI ECO PERFORMANCE (SEP) TECHNOLOGY

The Engine is powered by cutting edge Suzuki Eco Performance (SEP) technology which is the forward-looking engine development concept behind the creation of the powerplant for Access. The resulting engine delivers Superior Fuel Efficiency while also achieving Powerful Acceleration.

ONE WAY CLUTCH FOR QUIETER START

The engine starts up more quietly with roller type one-way clutch. The new clutch springs continue to allow engine starts at temperatures as low as -10°C.

LED HEAD LAMP

The LED headlight, framed by a chrome surround, not only enhances the scooter's aesthetic appeal but also delivers exceptional illumination.

NEW ICONIC POSITION LAMP

LED Position light comes in an iconic U-shaped design which makes it safer and easier for others to spot you on the road during day or night while still providing a stylish look.

CHROME FINISH MIRRORS

The reflective surface of a round-shaped mirror adds a touch of glamour, offering a blend of functionality and aesthetic appeal.

LED TAIL LAMP

The unique U-shaped LED tail lamp enhances the scooter's rear profile. The bright, energy-efficient LED lights illuminate the road behind you, ensuring optimal visibility, especially in low-light conditions.

FRONT LOCK OPERATED EXTERNAL FUEL LID

Refueling is made convenient with front lock operated external fuel lid on back. The lid features a dedicated space for the fuel cap, preventing it from being misplaced. The lid's design ensures that it cannot be closed if the cap is resting inside it, preventing accidental fuel leakage and ensuring safety.

ONE PUSH CENTRAL LOCK SYSTEM

For enhanced security, the scooter features a shutter key operation. A simple press of a button closes the shutter, while a magnet on the key opens it. For added convenience, the seat can also be accessed along the external fuel lid with the same lock.

ENGINE START & STOP SWITCH WITH EASY START SYSTEM

The integrated engine start-stop system, combined with the Suzuki Easy Start System, offers effortless ignition.

DUAL FRONT UTILITY POCKET

The dual front pockets provide a practical solution for storing small items like phones, keys, or portable chargers that cannot be left on the floorboard, ensuring quick and easy access.

FRONT USB CHARGING PORT

Stay connected on the go with the built-in USB charging port. Conveniently located near the front pocket, it allows you to easily place & charge your smartphone without the hassle of under seat charging. Not available in standard variant as standard fitment.

UNDER SEAT STORAGE

The scooter has large 24.4L under-seat storage which can easily accommodate a full-face helmet, shopping bags, purse and shoes can be stored and protected from rain and dust even while riding.

DUAL CONVENIENT UTILITY HOOKS

The scooter has two convenient utility hooks. A front hook is placed higher, so luggage doesn't drag on the floorboard when hanging.

TWO UNDER SEAT HOOKS

Two individual hooks at the front of the under-seat utility compartment make it easy to hang two helmets when parking by opening the seat, attaching the helmet straps, and then closing the seat.

SELF & KICK START CAPABILITY

Access has both Self & Kick start capability. For Electronic start, electromagnetic steel sheets are used in starter coil that respond faster compared to iron plates, which reduce mechanical loss and improve fuel efficiency. And for Kick start, condenser inside the regulator is provided that allows you to start scooter with a kick even if the battery dies.

TELESCOPIC FRONT SUSPENSION

Access uses a reliable telescopic front suspension and swingarm rear suspension for a smooth ride. The sliding performance is improved in both the suspensions.

LONG & ERGONIMIC SEAT

The wide and flat seat, combined with a sturdy grab rail, ensures comfort for both rider and pillion passengers. The narrower front section of the seat facilitates easy ground reach for the rider, while the premium leather-like seat cover adds a touch of sophistication.

SWINGARM REAR SUSPENSION

Access uses a reliable telescopic front suspension and swingarm rear suspension for a smooth ride. The sliding performance is improved in both the suspensions.

LONG FLOOR BOARD

The spacious floorboard offers ample room for comfortable riding, even with a pillion passenger. The three-dimensional and non-slip grids provide excellent grip, ensuring a secure footing.

PASSING SWITCH & HAZARD SWITCH

The scooter is equipped with passing and hazard switches, enhancing safety by improving visibility and alerting other road users.

PARKING BRAKE

A Brake lock lever is provided on the rear brake (left) lever. It secures the scooter when parked in inclined position.

ALUMINIUM FOOT REST

The die-cast aluminum footrest offers a premium look and feel. Its extended length and lowered position provide optimal comfort for the pillion rider. Additionally, the raised tip of the footrest facilitates easy mounting and dismounting.

SIDE STAND INTERLOCK

The Side Stand Interlock automatically cuts off the engine when the accelerator is pressed while the side stand is engaged. For added convenience, the Center Stand is redesigned to reduce the operating load by 23%, which reduces the effort to extend it.

FRONT DISC BRAKE

Access comes with front disc brake and it is also equipped with Combined Brake System (CBS) resulting in superior control and giving you a secure feeling all the time.

FRONT METAL FENDER & LEG SHIELD

To enhance durability, the front fender and leg shield are constructed from sturdy steel. This not only improves their resistance to damage but also simplifies repair processes.

12 INCH FRONT TYRE

The Access is equipped with tubeless tyres for enhanced safety and durability. The larger 12-inch front tyre improves handling.

LOW-ROLLING RESISTANCE REAR TYRE

The low-rolling-resistance rear tyre contributes to better fuel efficiency. Lightweight alloy wheels further enhance fuel efficiency and complete the stylish design.

HIGHLY RIGID FRAME

The Access features a lightweight yet highly rigid frame that contributes to increased fuel efficiency. The torsional rigidity is increased by 25%, maximizing strength and enhancing overall driving stability, both in straight lines and during cornering.
